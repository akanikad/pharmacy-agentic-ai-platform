# ADR-007: Buy vs. Build

## Status
Accepted

## Decision
Use managed/commodity services for capabilities that are not differentiating and build domain-specific orchestration, business rules, integrations, evaluation, and workflow controls.

## Decision criteria
1. Business differentiation
2. Security/compliance
3. Time to value
4. Total cost of ownership
5. Vendor lock-in
6. Operational burden
7. Integration fit

The goal is not to maximize custom code; it is to maximize business value while retaining control of the differentiating architecture.
